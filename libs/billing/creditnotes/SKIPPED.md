## Deviations kept in ported cases

- `create_from_termination_spec.rb:1096` expects 4.99 (precise 4.9949999). That value comes from the float day price `999.fdiv(30)`. The decimal day price is exactly 33.3, so 15 days come to 4.995, rounded to 5.00.
- `create_service_spec.rb:812` expects `higher_than_wallet_balance`, the specific code of a credit invoice item larger than the wallet.
- `create_service_spec.rb:825` checks that the field error is present, because the base checks are also reported.
